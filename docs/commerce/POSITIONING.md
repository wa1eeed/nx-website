Owner clarification, 2026-09-30: these pages sell technical solutions delivered
by NX. Market Builder must not read like a self-service tutorial or an account
sign-up product. This clarification supersedes the earlier self-service framing.

- Market Builder: NX configures the ready marketplace platform and available
  templates for the customer's brand. Explain solution components and outcomes.
- Marketplace Engine: NX scopes a tailored marketplace around specific workflows,
  permissions and integrations. Explain the requirements that justify this route.
- Both are delivered by NX. Do not differentiate them by saying one requires the
  customer to implement the technology. Do not invent different pricing, launch
  times, source-code ownership, scale limits or exclusivity of shared capabilities.
- Payment and settlement features support both solutions; they are not the reason
  to choose one over the other. Examples are illustrative, not client case studies.
- Provider integrations depend on confirmed scope and availability.
- Template editing, catalogue/category/page management and service-type support
  still require confirmation before adding explicit promises.
