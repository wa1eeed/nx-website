FAQ

Everything you need to know before you start.

How is commission calculated?

Commission is earned when your referral becomes an actual client who signs a contract through your link. The rate and its tiers depend on the type of service or solution, with full details shown in your dashboard and in the partner agreement upon joining.

When and how do I get paid?

Earnings are paid via regular payouts once conversions are confirmed and the minimum withdrawal threshold is met. The payout schedule, minimum and transfer method are fully explained in the partner agreement.

How long is a referral valid?

A client is attributed to you within the approved tracking window after their first click on your link, and the referral counts for you as long as they sign within that window. The exact duration is set out in the program terms.

Do I need technical experience?

No. We provide a ready-made marketing kit and clear explanations of every service and solution; you just connect the right client to us, and our team handles the rest of the technical and sales journey.

What can I promote?

The full NX portfolio: services (Launch, Grow, 360, Connect, Scale), solutions such as FinTech, and platforms and products such as IBP, Nqlah, NX Logistic and iWork. Refer whatever fits each client’s need.

How much does it cost to join?

Joining is completely free. No subscription fees and no upfront commitments — create your account and start marketing.
