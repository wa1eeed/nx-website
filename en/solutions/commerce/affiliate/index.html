Capabilities

The capabilities that power this solution

Split payments

A single transaction is divided automatically across several beneficiaries, each with a defined amount, without the platform first receiving the whole sum and then starting manual transfers.

Platform commission

Your platform takes its commission out of the same transaction at the moment of payment. This is your commercial revenue, separate from payment processing fees.

Merchant onboarding and verification (eKYC / KYB)

Instead of sending each seller off to open an account with the payment gateway and come back, your platform runs the whole onboarding journey: document upload, verification status tracking, and approval rules you can customise.

Sub-merchant accounts

Every seller becomes an independent sub-account under your platform instead of pooling everyone into one account, with payment methods, fees and settlements managed per merchant.

Balances and settlements

A clear financial picture for every seller: sales, pending, fees, refunds, commission, net earnings and settlements, with downloadable reports.

Payouts

Send earnings on demand or on a schedule to local bank accounts. Some transfers arrive instantly or near-instantly depending on the bank, the amount and the transfer channel.

Full and partial refunds

Return the full amount, or only the part that relates to a cancelled portion of the service, while the remainder stays owed to the people entitled to it.

Flexible fees and revenue sharing

Different pricing plans by merchant, package or sector: basic, professional and enterprise at a negotiated rate.
