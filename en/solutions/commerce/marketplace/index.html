Platform capabilities

What the marketplace can do

What gets switched on depends on the agreed scope and the approved payment provider, and is confirmed before launch.

Marketplace and catalogue

Catalogue and categories

The category tree and product attributes are built around what your marketplace actually sells — and what a seller may edit is set separately from what stays with you.

Search and filtering

Search inside the marketplace, filtered by category, price and seller. What surfaces and how results are ordered follows your priorities.

A page per seller

Each seller gets their own storefront inside your marketplace — products, ratings and policies — carrying your brand, not theirs.

Multi-seller cart and order

A customer buys from several sellers in one cart, and the order is divided between them at the moment of payment.

Ratings and reviews

Product or seller ratings once an order arrives, with publishing and reply rules set within scope.

Shipping and delivery

Shipping options and who carries the cost follow your model. Carrier integrations are assessed as part of the integration scope.

Coupons and offers

A discount across the marketplace, on one seller, or on one category — and who absorbs its cost is agreed before it goes live.

Returns and exchanges

The return window, who approves it and how it affects what is owed are all part of the workflow we build.

Payments and settlement

Commission per seller or per categoryPer-Merchant & Per-Category Commission

A different rate for each seller, or for each product category, rather than one rate across the whole marketplace. The rules are set within scope and depend on the provider.

Authorization and captureAuthorization & Capture

The amount is held on the customer’s card without being charged, then captured after delivery or completion — or released before settlement. This is not an escrow account and creates no fiduciary holding.

Splitting a paymentSplit Payment

One payment divided across several beneficiaries, with the shares summing to the transaction. Available for the models the provider has approved.

Full and partial refundsFull & Partial Refunds

Return the whole amount or part of it. How quickly it lands differs between mada and the international card schemes.

Payment methods

mada, Visa, Mastercard, digital wallets and SADAD. Further methods are enabled per seller, subject to availability with the provider.

Settlement

Each seller settles to their own verified bank account, and the marketplace share settles separately. Schedule and minimums follow the approved provider.

Recurring charges

Store the payment method to collect a subscription or periodic fee without re-entering the card.

Operations and control

Seller onboarding and verification

Sellers register inside your marketplace with their entity details and documents — and what is required differs by entity type: establishment, company, freelancer, non-profit.

A clear status for every seller

Under review, documents missing, partly active, active, on hold, rejected — shown to the seller with the reason and what will clear it.

A test environment before go-live

Every seller walks the order and payment journey in a sandbox before taking a real order.

Seller dashboard

Their orders, sales, balance, payouts and reports, in one place inside your marketplace.

Balances and reporting

Sales, pending, fees, refunds and net payable, with downloadable reports.

Fraud protection

3-D Secure, card tokenization and screening of suspicious transactions, as provided by the approved provider within the project scope.
